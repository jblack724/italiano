Formal and informal

Italian has two ways to say "you". Use tu with friends, family, classmates, and children. Use Lei (capital L) with strangers, older people, professors, and in shops.

  • Come stai? How are you? (informal)
  • Come sta? How are you? (formal)
  • Come ti chiami? What's your name? (informal)
  • Come si chiama? What's your name? (formal)

Ciao is informal for both hello and goodbye. With a professor, say buongiorno and arrivederci.

Introducing yourself

  • Mi chiamo Justin. My name is Justin.
  • Piacere! Nice to meet you!
  • Di dove sei? Where are you from? (informal)
  • Di dov'è? Where are you from? (formal)
