Topic heading (h4): formal and informal

Body text with Italian inline. Use tu with friends, family, and classmates. Use Lei (capital L) with strangers, older people, and professors.

  • Come stai? How are you? (informal)
  • Come sta? How are you? (formal)

Tip callout, for a rule of thumb or something the teacher stressed. Ciao is informal for both hello and goodbye.
